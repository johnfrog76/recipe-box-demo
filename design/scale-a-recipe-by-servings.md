# Scale a recipe by servings

> GitHub: https://github.com/johnfrog76/recipe-box-demo/issues/6

The conversation behind the spec. A recipe for four, cooked for six: every quantity times 1.5.

## What we talked about

- **Countable things round.** One and a half eggs is two eggs, and the step says so.
- **Pinches do not scale.** A pinch of salt for six is still a pinch.
- **Fractions stay fractions.** A third of a cup times 1.5 is half a cup, never 0.5.

## What we left out

Scaling cooking times. A bigger pot is not a longer bake in any rule we could write down.
